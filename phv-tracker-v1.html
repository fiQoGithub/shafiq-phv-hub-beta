How to use this tool

Made for PHV drivers. Everything stays on your phone.

Day check: is this rental or day worth it?
  1. Enter the rental, your usual gross fares, hours, fuel and other costs.
  2. Add the platform commission % and your targets per hour and per day.
  3. Read your take-home per hour, and the gross you need each day to hit both targets.

Tip: change the rental or fuel to compare two options before you sign.

My days: log what you really keep
  1. Pick the date and enter hours and km.
  2. Type your gross fares for each app you drove. Fee % is remembered after your first save.
  3. Add rental, fuel or charging, ERP and parking, and other costs, then save.

Gross means total fares per app, including cash trips the app records. Use Other for Ryde or jobs outside the apps. The top meter shows your last 7 days.

EV vs Petrol: which rental leaves you more?
  1. Enter your daily km and days per month.
  2. Fill in the petrol or hybrid car: fuel price, km per litre (a hybrid goes further on a litre, so use a higher number) and its daily rental.
  3. Fill in the EV: charging prices, kWh per 100 km, how much you charge in public, any charging discount and its daily rental.

The big number counts energy, rental and the time you lose charging. Use real prices from your charger app and rental quote, since the starting numbers are only examples.

Words used here

Gross: total fares before fees. Fee or commission: the % the app takes. Take-home: what you keep after the fee and all costs. ERP: road charges.
