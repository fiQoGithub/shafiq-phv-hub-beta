SHAFIQ PHV · LEARN & SHARE

Driver Knowledge Centre

A practical learning space for PHV drivers to build knowledge, adopt AI, create content, use technology and contribute to a stronger community.

OUR LEARNING PRIORITY

Learn AI. Create. Contribute. Grow.

Start with the basics of AI and ChatGPT. Then explore AI-assisted content creation, everyday productivity, PHV-related projects and ways to share useful knowledge. No coding experience required.

Join the PHV AI community

Explore the ecosystem — start anywhere

01 · PHV DRIVER ESSENTIALS

Understand the work behind the wheel

Learn about earnings, expenses, platform basics, vehicle decisions, daily operations and practical driver knowledge.

Goal: make better-informed decisions about your PHV work.

02 · AI LEARNING & ADOPTION

Become confident using AI tools

Learn AI foundations, ChatGPT, useful prompts, fact-checking, privacy and practical ways to use AI in everyday work and life.

Goal: move from hearing about AI to using it confidently.

03 · PHV CONTENT CREATOR PATHWAY

Turn your experience into useful content

Learn smartphone video basics, storytelling, TikTok ideas, hooks, scripts, captions and how AI can support your creative process. You do not need to become an influencer to benefit from sharing knowledge.

Goal: help drivers share authentic experiences and build digital skills.

04 · VEHICLE & TECHNOLOGY INTELLIGENCE

Understand vehicles and useful technology

Explore petrol, hybrid and EV comparisons, charging, digital tools and the costs behind vehicle decisions. Practical guidance will distinguish estimates from verified information.

05 · COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE & PROJECTS

Learn and build together

Share driver experiences, suggest improvements, contribute to voluntary surveys and help shape AI-assisted tools and resources that address real community needs.

06 · OPPORTUNITIES & COLLABORATION

Explore what we can build together

As the community grows, members may explore collaborative projects, creator opportunities and partnerships with relevant businesses and technology providers. Participation and commercial relationships will be transparent.

This area will grow with the community.

A community is more than a collection of lessons

You can learn, create content, share what you know, suggest a tool or contribute to a community project. Some members may be creators; others may prefer to learn, test tools or help solve practical problems. Every contribution can matter.
