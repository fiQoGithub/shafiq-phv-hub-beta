How the calculation works

Daily litres = daily kilometres ÷ km/L. At each station, purchase litres = daily litres + (round-trip detour kilometres ÷ km/L). Instant discount is applied to the eligible pump-price spend. Cashback is calculated on the post-discount payment, capped if applicable. Net daily cost = pump spend − instant discount − cashback. Monthly estimate = daily net cost × working days. Savings compare the two options for the same daily travel; extra detour fuel is charged to the station requiring the detour.

Fuel consumed on a detour may not equal fuel purchased that day. This simplified model treats detour consumption as an additional daily cost, useful for comparison rather than exact tank reconciliation.
